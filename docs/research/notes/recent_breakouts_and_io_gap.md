# Roblox 2025-2026 Non-Avatar Breakouts, .io Loops, Driving, Solo Hits

Snapshot date: 2026-10-13. Verification key: [F] = verified from a fetched page; [S] = search snippet only (not fetched). Trackers (RoWatcher, Rotrends, GGAID, RoVitals) disagree and use different windows; treat all CCU figures as approximate.

## Key question 1: 2025-2026 non-avatar breakouts at ~10K+ CCU

### Takeaway
No confirmed 2025-2026 Roblox game with the player as a non-avatar character was found at 10K+ CCU. The sources reviewed were not sufficient to rule it out completely, since few pages label the control scheme. Every 2025-2026 non-avatar-adjacent title found peaked well below 10K (see list), and the big 2025-2026 hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights, Brookhaven, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits, etc.) are avatar-based or not verified as non-avatar.

### Cited Findings

- Monster Survival (Antiael Games): released March 2025; all-time peak 207 (Aug 2026); 47 current; 4.8M visits; asymmetric Monster/Hunter/Survivor roles; the page does not say whether the player controls an animal — [F] [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7406477780/monster-survival)
- Snake Game (Studio Inked): launched Nov 2025; all-time peak 673 (May 2026) — [S] [RoWatcher via search](https://rowatcher.com/games/9260916865/snake-game)
- Be a Shark (Be a Shark Studios): launched Nov 2023; all-time peak 703 (May 2026) — [S] [RoWatcher via search](https://rowatcher.com/games/5289155961/be-a-shark)
- Build & Survive: released Sept 2025; peak 5.4K; theme not confirmed as animal — [S] [Rotrends via search](https://rotrends.com/game/8680465332/Build-Survive)
- Shark Escape! (Slippery Salamanders): released April 2025; all-time peak 45 — [S] [RoWatcher via search](https://rowatcher.com/games/7513796280/shark-escape-new)
- Build a Marble Slide! : listed as officially released Nov 11, 2025; a ball/marble-style game; 1.5M favorites and a 91.23% rating; no CCU given; the source's "last updated" date is inconsistent — [S] [Rotrends/third-party stats via search](https://rotrends.com/game/9150975254)
- Creatures of Sonaria: peak ~72.7K, 2.2B visits, but released June 2020 (outside window) — [S] [Rotrends via search](https://rotrends.com/game/1831550657/Creatures-of-Sonaria-Survive-Kaiju-Animals)

### Inferences
- The 2025-2026 non-avatar/creature segment appears to top out in the hundreds to low thousands of CCU based on the trackers found. The one 2025 creature-survival title with a usable figure (Trevor Henderson Creatures) peaked at 393.
- No 2025-2026 breakout in the animal, shark, or snake genres reached 10K in the sources found. The earlier research's list (Creatures of Sonaria, Dragon Adventures, Feather Family, Wild Horse Islands) remains the most recent confirmed set, and all of these predate 2025.

### Gaps
- No top-charts pull (roblox.com/charts, Rolimons, RoMonitor, RTrack) was fetched; the lists above come from search snippets and third-party pages.
- A March 2026 top-10 article (RoWatcher) did not state control schemes, so none of its top-10 titles could be classified as avatar or non-avatar from that source ([F] [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown)).
- Marble/ball, plane/ship, and morph-replacement games were not covered by any reliable 2025-2026 CCU source.
- "Steal An Egg" (claimed 1.6M CCU on Sept 30, 2026; 14.27M crossover peak Sept 19) appears only in a search snippet citing GGAID and RoVitals; not fetched and not verified. Its control scheme (avatar vs. egg-as-character) was not determined.
