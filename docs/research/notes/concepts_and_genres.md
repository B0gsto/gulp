# Concepts and Genres for a Non-Humanoid Roblox Game (as of October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Coverage is partial. About 13 tool calls were made. Several planned checks (RoMonitor, SteamDB/SteamSpy, DevForum postmortems, Roblox age-rating docs) were not completed. Gaps are listed per section.

## Key Question 1: Non-Roblox hits with non-humanoid characters that translate to Roblox

### Takeaway
Verified sales data is thin for most reference games. Untitled Goose Game is the only one with a sourced sales figure in this pass (1M copies within about 3 months of launch, Sept to Dec 2019). The strongest verified mobile signal is Hungry Shark (franchise passed 1B mobile downloads, reported Nov 2022). Creature and animal loops are the most relevant to Roblox, and Creatures of Sonaria is the best Roblox proof point.

### Cited Findings
- Untitled Goose Game (House House, 2019) passed 1 million sales about three months after its 20 Sept 2019 launch, per Panic's Cabel Sasser — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/news/untitled-goose-game-surpasses-1-million-copies-sold/); [Engadget, 31 Dec 2019](https://www.engadget.com/2019-12-31-untitled-goose-game-1-million-copies-sold.html)
- One OpenCritic item claims over 3M copies sold. It conflicts with the 1M reports and is treated as unreliable — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/1037/untitled-goose-game-reaches-major-milestone-in-three-months)
- Hungry Shark franchise passed 1 billion mobile downloads, reported Nov 2022, making it Ubisoft's most-downloaded mobile franchise — [Ubisoft press release via GamesPress](https://gamespress.com/HUNGRY-SHARK-FRANCHISE-HITS-ONE-BILLION-DOWNLOADS-ON-MOBILE-PLATFORMS); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/sleeper-hit-hungry-sharks-passes-1bn-lifetime-downloads-this-week)
- Hungry Shark World passed 10M downloads in its first 7 days (2016 launch) — [MCV/UK](https://www.mcvuk.com/development-news/hungry-shark-world-gobbles-up-10m-downloads-in-first-week/)
- Hungry Shark's growth is tied to a shift to a freemium model, with IAP for gold and gems central to monetization — [ifun.de review](https://www.ifun.de/vorsicht-bissig-hungry-shark-world-92087/). No revenue totals were found.
- Ubisoft is reported to be consulting on closing Ubisoft London (formerly Future Games of London), with Ubisoft Barcelona Mobile taking over the franchise. The article is undated in the results — [LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/noticias/ubisoft-cerrara-el-estudio-encargado-de-una-saga-con-1000-millones-de-descargas/)
- No sales figures were found for Stray, Donut County, Katamari Damacy, Little Kitty Big City, Maneater, Goat Simulator, Bee Simulator, Trackmania, Rocket League, Slither.io, or Agar.io. These were not verified in this pass.

### Inferences
- Hungry Shark's loop (a non-humanoid creature grows by eating, with unlockable creatures and a freemium layer) maps directly to Roblox. It is a mobile-first, short-session, progression-driven loop, which matches Roblox's audience.
- Goose-style "chaotic physical sandbox" games are better suited to a single-player PC/console design than to Roblox's social, short-session audience. Adapting them would mean building a social or PvP layer on top.
- Verified data is too thin to rank these reference games by commercial success. Treat the rankings below as qualitative.

### Gaps
- Sales or revenue for Stray, Donut County, Katamari Damacy, Little Kitty Big City, Maneater, Goat Simulator, Bee Simulator, Trackmania, Rocket League, Slither.io, Agar.io. Next step: SteamDB/SteamSpy/Gamalytic and Sensor Tower/data.ai for mobile.
- Current Hungry Shark revenue (only downloads were found).

## Key Question 2: Which loops already exist on Roblox, how they perform, and where the gaps are

### Takeaway
Creature and animal loops already exist on Roblox and are well established. Creatures of Sonaria is the strongest proof point. The io/snake-style loop has Roblox clones that are small or unverified, which suggests a gap in quality. Pet and animal simulators are large by live count but crowded.

### Cited Findings
- Creatures of Sonaria (Twin Atlas, creator Mary/Erythia) had about 26,000 concurrent players at press time, per Tubefilter (date not stated in the excerpt) — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=194084)
- Creatures of Sonaria is top of Rotrends' animal sim list at 2.11B visits, with about 37,769 current players listed (snapshot date not stated) — [Rotrends animal sim list](https://rotrends.com/games/genre%3Asub%3AAnimal%20Sim)
- Twin Atlas signed a TV adaptation deal with Wind Sun Sky Entertainment and Productivity Media. Sources disagree on the date (Feb 2021 vs Feb 2023) — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/creatures-of-sonaria-partners-with-wind-sun-sky-entertainment-to-create-series-adaption-roblox/)
- Roblox's own developer-spotlight series profiled Mary (Creatures of Sonaria) under "Game Changers" — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=194084)
- June 2026 live-player snapshot (RoWatcher): Adopt Me! about 324,000 playing (2nd overall); Pet Simulator 99 about 78,000 (14th) — [RoWatcher ranking](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-most-popular-roblox-games-right-now-ranked-by-live-players)
- Smaller animal sims show 74 players online each (RoWatcher snapshot, undated): Animal Race Simulator (peak 222) and Animal Kingdom Animal Sim (peak 208) — [Animal Race Simulator](https://rowatcher.com/games/5940874374/animal-race-simulator); [Animal Kingdom](https://rowatcher.com/games/4721240977/animal-kingdom-animal-sim)
- A Roblox Slither.io-style game exists: Hungry Worm Slither.io Clone, a fan game listed on itch.io — [itch.io](https://kyranok.itch.io/hungry-worm-slitherio-clone). Slither Simulator is a Roblox listing with non-stop PvP — [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/8597844216/Slither-Simulator). Player counts for both were not found.
- No Roblox game clearly matching Hungry Shark was found in this pass.
- Roblox's 2024 genre research (Q1 2024 search volumes) flagged driving/racing (~12.5M searches), sports (13.5M), open world action (14M), and social co-opetition (7.5M). It called horror "extremely high growth" without a figure. It named Creatures of Sonaria as a success — [Roblox Genre Insights, July 2024](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2024/07/roblox-genre-insights-what-will-you-create-next)

### Inferences
- Creature survival (Creatures of Sonaria) has proven demand on Roblox. The bar for quality is high, and an incumbent with a large catalog already exists, so a new entrant needs a distinct hook.
- An io-style or Hungry-Shark-style growth loop does not have a clear, high-quality Roblox incumbent based on the evidence found. This is an inference from a thin search, not a verified market gap.
- Animal-themed simulators have many entries but low concurrent players per game, which suggests saturation at the low end.

### Gaps
- No RoMonitor, Rolimons or RTrack data was accessed. Visit counts, favorites and CCU were not checked for io, Hungry Shark or pet-sim titles.
- No verified player counts for the Hungry Worm or Slither Simulator Roblox games.
- Creatures of Sonaria's current CCU and visits need a dated source.

## Key Question 3: Retention and monetization mechanics on top Roblox games (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced mechanic is gacha/rarity-driven collection, shown by the brainrot trend. The most reliable monetization evidence is for 18+ users, who monetize at higher rates. Limited-time events and short-spike dynamics also appear in the data.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot peaked at about 25.8M concurrent players in Oct 2025. Outlets report 25.2M to 25.8M, so the exact figure varies — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Outlook India](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-breaks-all-time-player-record)
- RoWatcher says Steal a Brainrot had fallen to about 215,000 CCU (about 99.2% below peak), with undated publication. Escape Tsunami For Brainrots (briefly #1 in Jan 2026) was about 164,000. Swing Obby for Brainrots fell from 87,000 peak to about 57,000 within about five weeks — [RoWatcher analysis](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- Many "Stealing From Brainrots" clones peaked at about 3K CCU (Feb 2026) — same RoWatcher source family, see [RoWatcher game page](https://rowatcher.com/games/9590247304/stealing-from-brainrots)
- Q1 2026 (shareholder letter, via search summary; not opened directly): over-18 users make up 26% of age-checked DAUs. In the US, 18+ DAUs and hours grew about 40% YoY, and 18+ users monetized over 50% higher than under-18s — [Roblox Q1 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/Q1-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- Q1 2026: total DAUs 132M, up 35% YoY, with growth slowing from about 70% in the prior two quarters. The full-year revenue guide was cut to 20-25% after age checks reduced chat, signups and DAU — same letter, via search summary
- Hungry Shark's freemium IAP model (gems/gold) is the mobile analogue for creature-progression monetization — [ifun.de](https://www.ifun.de/vorsicht-bissig-hungry-shark-world-92087/)

### Inferences
- Brainrot-style collection and rarity loops drive huge spikes, but the decline is steep and event-driven. A creature game needs ongoing content cadence and a retention layer beyond the first spike.
- The 18+ monetization gap suggests that a creature game with mature-leaning progression or collection could monetize better, but the age-check and maturity rules limit this (see Risks).
- Roblox's shift to age-checked audiences means chat and social features may need to work with age-checked accounts.

### Gaps
- Specific mechanics (offline progression, trading, prestige) not sourced in this pass. Each needs a Roblox-specific source (DevForum, Naavik, GDC).
- Bloomberg's ~$64M real-money purchases figure for Steal a Brainrot appeared in a search summary only. It is unverified and has no fetched link.
- A profitable.app figure of $574.6M is a modeled estimate and is not reliable.

## Key Question 4: Realistic solo MVP scope and update cadence

### Takeaway
No sourced solo-developer postmortem for a 2025 Roblox hit was found. Scope estimates in this section are unsourced and need verification.

### Cited Findings
- The DevForum postmortem search returned only generic SEO pages, with no named solo developer, game or post — [search, see Gaps]
- The ROLearn Q2 2026 report gives development timelines of 4-6 months for co-op survival and 6-12 months for sports, with no sources — [ROLearn](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/emerging-roblox-game-genres-q2-2026)
- Roblox Jumpstart (GDC 2026) targets newcomers. Its genre focus is "fresh takes on RPG, shooter, strategy, sports, or other genres 18+ players love" — [Roblox Jumpstart](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart); [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/roblox-incubator-jumpstart-creator-programs/)

### Inferences
- A 6-10 week solo MVP is plausible only for a narrow loop: one creature or object, one map, one progression track, and a small set of collectibles. This is an inference, not a sourced figure.
- Jumpstart's genre focus suggests a possible program fit for an RPG/strategy/shooter version of a creature concept, but terms, eligibility and funding were not checked.

### Gaps
- No verified content counts at launch or update cadence for solo Roblox hits.
- Jumpstart eligibility, funding and deadlines were not checked (Roblox Jumpstart page only partly read).

## Key Question 5: 2026 trends for Roblox's audience

### Takeaway
Animals, pets and brainrot-style collection are established but fading in peak numbers. Older-audience and sports, racing, RPG and narrative genres show stated demand, but the most cited figures are dated or from unverified third parties.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox Q1 2026: DAUs 132M, up 35% YoY (growth slowing) — [Q1 2026 letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/Q1-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- Roblox says RPG, strategy and shooter genres are "heavily underrepresented" despite older-player demand, per the GDC 2026 Jumpstart/Incubator announcement — [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/roblox-incubator-jumpstart-creator-programs/)
- ROLearn (March 2026, third party): story/narrative search terms up 65% QoQ; co-op survival rising; music/rhythm and educational under-supplied. The report names no games and gives no methodology — [ROLearn](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/emerging-roblox-game-genres-q2-2026)
- Brainrot trend: record in Oct 2025, steep 2026 decline, with newer brainrot games (Escape Tsunami, Swing Obby) growing and dropping quickly — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)

### Inferences
- The "underserved" signals (RPG, strategy, shooter, sports, racing, open world) align with a creature or vehicle character that fits an open-world or action frame. This is an inference from Roblox's own stated gaps.
- Roblox's stated 2024 interest in open world action and driving is not yet confirmed by 2026 data.

### Gaps
- No 2026 Roblox-official genre report was found. The most recent official genre data is from July 2024.
- Horror, survival and cosy trends were not checked against 2026 CCU data.
- The 17+ and older audience growth is documented in the Q1 2026 letter, but the Q2 2026 letter (about late July 2026) was not checked.

## Key Question 6: Risks

### Takeaway
Roblox's paid random item rules (odds disclosure, regional restrictions, spending limits for younger accounts) are the most concrete compliance risk for a gacha-style loop. The age-check and chat changes affect social features.

### Cited Findings
- Paid random items require all outcomes and odds listed as percentages that total exactly 100%. Info buttons need a descriptive label, and odds apply to indirect purchases too. Source: [Roblox Creator Hub, paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items) (seen via search summary; full page not fetched)
- Certain paid random item requirements may depend on country regulation. A PolicyService flag, ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted, is checked per player — [Roblox Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items); [NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/articles/why-games-change-by-country) (secondary source). The list of restricted countries was not found.
- In May 2026, a petition called for an investigation into child safety and in-app spending on Roblox. Roblox said it has rules on odds disclosure and spending limits for younger accounts — search-summary source, see [Accred DEAC page](https://accred.deac.org/expert-time/US-Advocacy-Groups-Call-for-Investigation-into-Roblox-Child-Safety-and-InApp-Spending-Practices-19-1279) (low-quality source)
- Global age checks for chat reduced communication, organic signups and DAU in Q1 2026 — [Q1 2026 letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/Q1-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- Creatures of Sonaria has a TV adaptation deal, which shows a Roblox creature IP can be licensed out. This is evidence that the category attracts outside attention, not a risk finding.

### Inferences
- A creature game with a gacha/egg-hatch loop must include odds disclosure and a regional-restriction check, or it risks removal.
- Adapting Goose, Hungry Shark or Stray mechanics is fine. Using their names, characters, art or specific level designs is the IP risk. Stick to genre mechanics, and do not use derivative names or assets.

### Gaps
- Copycat speed on Roblox was not researched. The Steal a Brainrot wave and its clones are evidence of fast copying, but no timing data was found.
- Roblox age rating and content questionnaire (maturity labels) were not checked. The user needs these before choosing a maturity level for a creature game.
- Full text of the Roblox paid random items and virtual items policy pages was not read, only search summaries.

## Recommended Concepts (for downstream synthesis, not final)

Based only on the evidence above, these are the best-supported directions. Each is an original concept, not a clone.

1. **Creature growth/evolution io-style game** (Hungry Shark and io-loop mechanics, non-humanoid creature). Support: Hungry Shark's mobile record, Hungry Worm and Slither Simulator as Roblox signals. Risk: no verified Roblox incumbent quality data yet. Scope: narrow MVP is plausible.
2. **Creature collection/survival with an open-world or vehicle hook** (Creatures of Sonaria-adjacent, but original). Support: Creatures of Sonaria's demand and Roblox's underserved-genre signals for open world and racing. Risk: strong incumbent. Needs a distinct hook. Scope: higher than option 1.
3. **Object/vehicle sports or racing party game** (ball, car or cart as the character). Support: Roblox's 2024 demand for driving and sports. Risk: crowded genre, and no 2026 data confirming demand. Scope: moderate.

Priority verification before committing: RoMonitor/Rolimons CCU for the io and creature games above, Roblox Q2 2026 letter, and a DevForum or GDC postmortem from a solo Roblox developer.
