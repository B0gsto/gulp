# Successful Roblox Non-Avatar Games (research as of 2026-10-13)

Scope: Roblox experiences where the player controls a non-humanoid character (animal, creature, dragon, ball, marble, dinosaur, horse, duck, etc.). Vehicle games are noted separately because the player usually still has a humanoid avatar.

Data quality warning: almost all concurrent-user (CCU) and visit figures below come from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, Rotrends, Bloxodes, Profitable, RoLearn). Snapshot dates are mostly not shown, and the trackers disagree with each other by 20-50% on the same game. Figures marked "tracker" are unverified. Revenue, team size, and monetization were NOT verified for almost any title. Only one primary-ish source (Coolabi press release, 2023) and one company source (Roblox Q2 2026 results, via secondary reporting) were found.

Research constraints: about 14 tool calls, a short search pass, and no direct page fetches. Several items are gaps and need follow-up.

---

## Q1. Which notable experiences replace the avatar entirely?

### Takeaway
Non-avatar animal and creature sims are a proven, large category on Roblox. Creatures of Sonaria, Feather Family, Dragon Adventures, Wild Horse Islands, and Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition each show hundreds of millions to over a billion lifetime visits (tracker figures). The category is a mature, multi-year genre with several incumbents, not a new niche. Most of the hard metrics (revenue, team size) are still unverified.

### Cited Findings

**Creatures of Sonaria** (non-humanoid: players are fantastical animals; Kaiju-themed survival/creature sim)
- Launch: June 25, 2020 (most sources); one blog claims a 2019 founding, unverified. Source: [rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/1831550657); [profitable](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/creatures-of-sonaria-survive-kaiju-animals); [alpha.cireba](https://alpha.cireba.com/spotlightssports4/unveiling-creatures-of-sonaria-goskogur-events-more.html) (unreliable, contradicts on 2019)
- Developer: Sonar Studios, a Roblox group. One listing says Sonar publishes 24 games. Source: [profitable](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/creatures-of-sonaria-survive-kaiju-animals); [shapes.inc fandom](https://shapes.inc/fandom/creatures-of-sonaria) (low reliability)
- Sonar Studios later merged with RedManta to form Twin Atlas (Roblox-focused studio, CEO Samuel Garcia). Twin Atlas signed a TV series adaptation deal with Wind Sun Sky Entertainment and Productivity Media. Source: [Pryor Cashman](https://www.pryorcashman.com/film-tv-podcast/news/pryor-cashman-client-twin-atlas-signs-roblox-tv-series-deal); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=194084)
- Lifetime visits: about 1.87B (one tracker), 2.1B (another), 1.5B+ (2026 comparison article). Favorites about 9.5M. Source: [earnaldo 2026 comparison](https://earnaldo.com/blog/creatures-of-sonaria-vs-adopt-me); [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1831550657)
- CCU: snapshots of about 25,080 (one tracker, +13.7% in 24h, linked to Spring Meadows event), 37.2K (another page, timestamp unclear), and 27.8K (earnaldo 2026 comparison). Typical range quoted as 10K-30K. Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1831550657); [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/creatures-of-sonaria-vs-adopt-me)
- Monetization: not verified in this pass (likely skins, pets/species, and game passes; needs confirmation).

**Dragon Adventures** (non-humanoid: dragon)
- Lifetime visits: 1.54B (RoWatcher, exact count 1,535,723,041), about 1.7B (Rotrends, Profitable). Favorites about 12.5M. Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1235188606)
- CCU: 11,104 (RoWatcher), 9.6K (Profitable), about 8.9K 7-day average (Rotrends). All-time peak 61K per Rotrends. Rotrends ranks it #182 globally. Source: [rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/1235188606/NEW-Dragon-Adventures); [profitable](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/dragon-adventures)
- Multiple versioned listings ([NEW], [REBUILD], [EVENT], [LEGO]) suggest the game runs a long-lived, regularly rebuilt, updated format. Launch year, developer, and team size: GAP.

**Feather Family** (non-humanoid: muscovy duck; animal sim)
- Lifetime visits about 1.4B (Bloxodes ~1.4B, Rotrends 1.42B), 2.2M favorites. Source: [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/feather-family-muscovy-duck-547524060)
- CCU: 11K 24h peak (Bloxodes, undated); 9,880 current (Rotrends, undated). Source: [rotrends](https://rotrends.com/games/genre%3Asub%3AAnimal%20Sim)
- A separate "Feather Family Classic" experience has about 7.4M visits. Source: [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/feather-family-classic-1795547191)
- Developer and launch year: GAP.

**Wild Horse Islands** (non-humanoid: horse; open world horse sim)
- Lifetime visits: about 670M (one page), 617M (July 2026 comparison). Source: [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=119986); [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/horse-life-vs-wild-horse-islands)
- CCU: 13K-14K current (undated snapshots); all-time peak 25,026, with servers capped at 20 players. Source: [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=119986); earnaldo comparison above
- Rating about 92% (July 2026 comparison). Developer, launch year, team size: GAP.

**Horse Life** (non-humanoid: horse; developer listed as Sonar Studios in one comparison)
- CCU: about 3,600 (June 2026 comparison); about 3K current with all-time peak 21K in September 2024 (tracker). Lifetime visits 347M (June 2026) to 362.2M (tracker). Source: [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/horse-life-vs-wild-horse-islands); [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/5422546686/horse-life)
- Note: Horse Life's CCU fell from a 21K peak (Sept 2024) to about 3K in mid-2026 per these snapshots, while Wild Horse Islands kept a much higher count. This is a comparison of two snapshots, not a verified trend.

**Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition** (non-humanoid: cats; licensed from the Warrior Cats book series, published by Coolabi)
- Coolabi press release (May 9, 2023, second anniversary): 300M visits since launch, top 100 on Roblox for gameplay time and retention, more than 2M unique players per month. Source: [coolabi](https://coolabi.com/warrior-cats-ultimate-edition-roblox-game-hits-300-million-game-visits-on-its-second-anniversary/) (primary, company-issued, dated 2023)
- Later tracker snapshot: about 718M visits, 4,194 CCU (undated). Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1278035280)
- Licensed IP, which is an important difference from the other titles here. Revenue and team size: GAP.

**Prior Extinction** (non-humanoid: dinosaur survival; Jacys Studios)
- Visits: 97.6M (Bloxodes), 105.3M (Rotrends), over 90.8M (Earnaldo June 2026). Favorites about 324K-340K.
- CCU: 24h peak 3.1K (Bloxodes); peak 8.6K (Rotrends); about 1,200 concurrent (Earnaldo, June 2026). Source: [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/prior-extinction-dinosaur-survival-2527789729); [rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/2527789729); [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/prior-extinction-free-robux-guide)
- Mid-scale: about one-tenth of Creatures of Sonaria's CCU by the same sources.

**Kaiju Universe**: only a generic description was found (playable kaiju versus human factions). No stats, developer, or date. GAP.

**Dinosaur Simulator**: no reliable results. GAP.

**Super Blocky Ball** (non-avatar: a ball; marble/obstacle game by Maelstronomer, published June 2014)
- Visits about 64.3M (RoWatcher), favorites about 451K, rating 88.4% (Bloxodes). Live count 8, all-time peak 25 (July 2026). Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/77610935/super-blocky-ball); [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/super-blocky-ball-77610935)
- Spinoff SBB Marathon: 1.7M visits, all-time peak 26 (July 2026). Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1722481076/sbb-marathon)
- Shows a long-lived but small-CCU ball/marble game. Monetization and team size: GAP.

**Marble Mania** (non-avatar marble; moonbeam; released on Roblox April 27, 2020)
- Rank #10231 (Rotrends), peak 547 CCU, 7-day average about 54, 87.6M visits, rating 91.3%. Source: [rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/1699709637)
- Speedrun community exists. The search could not confirm a clean link between the marble title and this game. Treat as partially verified.

### Inferences
- Animal/creature sims (Sonaria, Dragon Adventures, Feather Family, Wild Horse Islands) hold up well on lifetime visits (roughly 0.6B to 2B), but tracker CCU shows an unstable and wide range. Recent CCU is roughly 3K-30K per title.
- Pure ball/marble games are much smaller (Super Blocky Ball peak in the 20s in July 2026 snapshots), which suggests they are a cheap but low-ceiling niche.
- Licensed IP (Warrior Cats) or a studio with multiple titles (Sonar Studios, 24 games) is one plausible pattern for these hits. Not verified.

### Gaps
- Launch year, developer, and team size are missing for Dragon Adventures, Feather Family, Wild Horse Islands, and Kaiju Universe.
- Monetization breakdown (skins, species, gacha, game passes, private servers) was not verified for any title.
- No revenue estimates with a method were found.
- Dinosaur Simulator and Kaiju Universe could not be found in trackers.
- Wolves' Life: no results found under this name. Search by exact title or developer is needed.
- Fish It, Ship/Driving Submarine, and Fisch-style games were not checked for this pass.

## Q2. Are there 2025-2026 breakouts where you play as a non-humanoid?

### Takeaway
No 2025-2026 non-humanoid breakout was confirmed in this pass. The strongest 2025 breakout found, 99 Nights in the Forest, is a humanoid survival game. Non-avatar titles found here are mostly 2014-2024 launches. This is a real gap.

### Cited Findings
- 99 Nights in the Forest (Grandma's Favourite Games, Alec Kieft / Cracky4): passed 1B visits months after its 2025 release. Humanoid survival game. Source: [GamesBeat/RDC 2026 coverage via search summary](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-hits-20-and-opens-rdc-2026-to-invite-the-world-to-play-and-make-games-dave-baszucki-keynote/); [GDC 2026 speaker page](https://schedule.gdconf.com/speaker/kieft-alec/80898) (confirms the speaker, not the 1B figure; 1B visits claim not independently verified)
- Driving Empire (Voldex): 38,408 CCU and 2.71B lifetime visits (tracker, undated). Vehicle game; the player still has a humanoid avatar, so it is not strictly non-avatar. Official page shows aircraft and carrier additions dated July 10, 2026. Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1202096104/driving-empire-dodge-update); [roblox.com](https://www.roblox.com/games/3351674303)
- A GDC 2026 Roblox session stated that teams of fewer than 10 are reaching 25M+ concurrent users with social loops and modern tech. Source: summary from search results (primary session page not fetched; treat as unverified)
- Roblox says it is launching a developer video series to boost recently released games (Tubefilter). Source: [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=194084)

### Inferences
- The 2025-2026 growth driver found in these sources is social loops and fast-iterating humanoid experiences, not non-humanoid formats.
- Creature sims are a mature format with stable incumbents. Newer entrants would compete with titles that have 1B+ lifetime visits.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025 or 2026 non-avatar launch with trackable growth. Search Roblox trending lists and RoStatus/RoWatcher "new and rising" for 2025-2026 titles.
- Growth drivers (update cadence, events, influencer, UGC) not verified for any non-avatar title in 2025-2026.

## Q4. Notable failures or declines among non-avatar games

### Takeaway
Platform-level decline is well sourced. A non-avatar title-level failure example was not confirmed. Horse Life's CCU fall (21K peak Sept 2024 to about 3K mid-2026, tracker) is the only title-level decline found.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox Q2 2026: daily active users 123M, down from a 152M peak in Q3 2025, and still up about 10% year over year. Monthly unique players about 27M (down from about 37M two quarters earlier). Source: [Interspace Music summary](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy) (secondary reporting; check the Roblox Q2 2026 shareholder letter)
- Management guided to a 14-18% year-over-year decline in Q3 2026 (secondary reporting). Stock reportedly fell about 27-30% after Q2 earnings on July 30-31, 2026 (sources disagree on date and percent; the "70%" figure appears mainly in smaller outlets). Source: [Comic Shop Locator](https://www.comicshoplocator.com/2026/08/10/roblox-shares-70-percent-drop-player-decline-ai-pivot-2026/); [gaminghq](https://gaminghq.eu/2026/08/07/roblox-stock-crashes-players-reject-microtransactions/)
- Causes as stated by the company: CFO said players are "playing the wrong games" and cited a lack of "vintage viral games" and a shift toward lower hourly monetization. CEO attributed the DAU trend mainly to seasonality. A June 2026 recommendation-engine change (28-day behavior window instead of 7-day) and age-check/restricted chat rules are cited as possible factors by one source. Source: [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy); [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/) (causal claims are secondary and not verified)
- Horse Life CCU: about 21K all-time peak (September 2024) to about 3K (tracker, undated mid-2026). Source: [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/5422546686/horse-life)

### Inferences
- The CFO's "vintage viral games" framing suggests that a platform-wide demand gap exists for viral, low-monetization-per-hour titles. This is relevant to a non-avatar game but is an interpretation of management commentary.
- A non-avatar game's risk is therefore less about the avatar format and more about platform-wide DAU softness.

### Gaps
- No documented post-mortem of a failed non-avatar Roblox game was found.
- The Roblox Q2 2026 shareholder letter was not fetched; figures above are secondary.

## Commercial viability notes (as of 2026-10-13)

- Non-avatar animal, creature, and dragon sims have high lifetime visits (about 0.6B-2B) across several incumbents. Recent CCU is roughly 3K-30K per title (tracker, mixed dates).
- Roblox platform DAU is down from its Q3 2025 peak (152M to 123M in Q2 2026, secondary). A new game in a crowded genre faces both platform softness and entrenched incumbents.
- Revenue estimates (DevEx, Bloxodes, Rotrends) were not verified. Do not use per-title revenue figures from this file without verification.
