# Art and Animation References: Stylized Underwater Creature Game

Collected notes only. No design decisions are made here.
"Verified" means the URL appeared in web search results during this session. Pages were not fetched in full. "Unverified" means no working URL was found in search results.

Target look: chunky readable cartoon creatures, bright saturated reef, soft lighting, mobile-friendly low-poly with hand-painted or flat-shaded textures. Not Roblox-style blocky avatars.

---

## Art references

### 1. ABZU (Giant Squid, 2016)
- URL: https://www.killscreen.com/you-had-me-beautiful-underwater-game-journeys-art-director (verified, press coverage; also https://80.lv/articles/interview-with-the-creative-director-of-abzu/)
- Borrow: Underwater light shafts and haze as the main mood tool. Swaths of colour gradient from sunlit blue to deep teal. Fish and sea life read as simplified, graceful shapes rather than detailed models.
- Do not copy: The zen, sparse, mostly-empty ocean pacing and the 3D diver-focused camera.

### 2. Subnautica (Unknown Worlds, 2018)
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/264710/Subnautica/ (verified)
- Borrow: Depth-based colour falloff (warm shallows to cold dark blue), reef-like coral silhouettes, bioluminescent accents against dark water.
- Do not copy: Its survival-horror tone and creature detail level. Its flashing-light warning is a reminder to keep effects restrained.

### 3. Tasty Blue (Dingo Games, 2D eat-em-up)
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/345200 (verified)
- Borrow: The core "eat smaller, grow, get eaten by bigger" loop in a readable underwater side view. Playable fish with clear size differences.
- Do not copy: The flat 2D sprite look; the goal is 3D low-poly.

### 4. Hungry Shark World (Ubisoft Sydney / Ubisoft, mobile)
- URL: https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/games/hungry-shark-world (verified)
- Borrow: Exaggerated, cartoony shark and prey proportions. Bold, instantly readable prey types for a mobile audience. Fast feedback when eating.
- Do not copy: Its ad-driven shop and cluttered UI. Ubisoft describes content as "bite-size fish to whales and unwitting humans"; the human-eating theme is not a model here.

### 5. Dave the Diver (Mintrocket, 2023)
- URL: https://themarysue.com/dave-the-diver-is-blowing-up-on-steam-in-the-most-unexpected-way/ (verified, press article). Steam page and launch trailer not verified.
- Borrow: Pixel-and-3D hybrid presentation. Character silhouettes that stay clear at small size. Cinematic cut-scenes and a warm, saturated palette.
- Do not copy: The pixel texture treatment, since the target is hand-painted or flat-shaded low-poly.

### 6. Another Crab's Treasure (Aggro Crab, 2024)
- URL: https://intoindiegames.com/features/another-crabs-treasure-looks-to-bring-some-color-to-the-soulslike-realm-without-compromising-on-darkness/ (verified, press feature). Launch-coverage also at https://gamingtrend.com/news/another-crabs-treasure-a-nautical-souls-like-adventure-is-out-today
- Borrow: Colourful underwater palette with sea-life characters that have distinct shell and body shapes. Art director Nick Kaman cites Nintendo platformers as inspiration for readability.
- Do not copy: The grim soulslike framing and dark mood. The colour work is the useful part.

### 7. Maneater (Tripwire Interactive, 2020)
- URL: https://www.gameinformer.com/index.php/2020/05/18/tripwire-interactive-unleashes-the-maneater-launch-trailer (verified, article with launch trailer)
- Borrow: Shark silhouette and turning animation at speed. Light shafts and clear-water colour in shallows. Clear prey variety.
- Do not copy: Gore, the realistic anatomy, and the grim revenge-combat tone.

### 8. Luca (Pixar, 2021)
- URL: https://www.slashfilm.com/580701/pixar-luca-making-of/ (verified, making-of article)
- Borrow: Simplified forms and clear silhouettes for water and creatures. Gestural, "hand of the artist" line quality. Sinuous reflections on water. Ghibli and woodblock-print influences.
- Do not copy: The human character designs and the coastal-village setting.

### 9. Alba: A Wildlife Adventure (ustwo games, 2020)
- URL: https://ustwogames.co.uk/news/the-environment-art-of-alba-a-wildlife-adventure/ (verified, environment-art post by Jessie van Aelst)
- Borrow: Saturated warm palette, rounded forms, and block-colour simplification with clear focal points.
- Do not copy: The island and land-based setting. Review notes that the models are minimally detailed with round edges and block colours, which matches the target.

### 10. Sea of Stars (Sabotage Studio, 2023)
- URL: https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/sea-of-stars-gets-new-trailer-as-demo-releases-for-pc-via-steam (verified, press article; Steam demo mentioned)
- Borrow: Dynamic lighting and a day/night colour cycle layered over a stylized world. Creature and enemy readability in a bright world.
- Do not copy: The pixel art itself.

### 11. Splatoon 3 (Nintendo, 2022)
- URL: https://splatoon.nintendo.com/en/news/ask-the-developer-vol-7-splatoon-3-part-1/ (verified, Nintendo developer interview)
- Borrow: Bright, saturated ink-colour palette with a simple, instantly readable squid form. The original prototype's cube-shaped "blocks of tofu" showed how far a stylized shape can be pushed before it reads.
- Do not copy: The street-fashion human characters and the heavy UI graphic style.

### 12. Pikmin 4 (Nintendo, 2023)
- URL: https://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-10-pikmin-4-part-1 (verified, Nintendo developer interview)
- Borrow: Simple, leaf-headed silhouettes that read at a glance. Colour and marking used to signal each creature's role. Cute with a slight eerie edge (artist Junji Morii cites Tim Burton).
- Do not copy: The top-down squad-command interface.

### 13. Fisch (Roblox, 2024)
- URL: https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Fisching/Fisch (verified, wiki page). The art claims below come from a comparison blog, not the wiki. The Roblox Wiki itself does not describe the art style.
- Borrow: Roblox-native underwater lighting that changes with weather and time of day, atmospheric fog, caustic patterns, and underwater particles. This shows what a Roblox underwater scene can reach.
- Do not copy: Its blocky avatars, fishing-loop UI, and generic Roblox character look. The wiki's developer attribution conflicts with another source (WoozyNate vs. FineFish Studios).

### 14. Creatures of Sonaria (Roblox)
- URL: Unverified. Only low-quality SEO blogs surfaced in search; no official Roblox page URL was found.
- Borrow (low confidence, from SEO blogs only): Large expressive eyes, soft colour palettes, whimsical creature shapes, and smooth animation. The sources disagree on whether the Ghibli comparison is official.
- Do not copy: The unverified blog claims as design fact. Check the official game page and developer posts directly.

### 15. KOI (Dotoyou Games, Steam)
- URL: https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/award-winning-game-koi-heading-to-steam/ (verified, news article with embedded trailer; trailer link not captured)
- Borrow: Small koi protagonist with a clean, readable silhouette in a lotus-pond setting. The pond is a compact, watery environment.
- Do not copy: Its puzzle-adventure structure. Visual detail beyond the protagonist is not described in the search results, so treat specifics as unverified.

## Articles and talks on stylized underwater rendering and creature readability

### A. Stylized Underwater Environment (Unity, 2018), Karl Schecht
- URL: https://karlschecht.artstation.com/projects/vg2kY (verified, ArtStation project page)
- Use: Stylized underwater scene built without custom shaders. Its flashing additive particle-plane caustics, rotated with the sun, are a practical technique for fake caustics.

### B. Caustics water texturing in Unity3D, Dual Heights
- URL: https://dualheights.se/caustics/caustics-water-texturing-using-unity3d.shtml (verified, tutorial)
- Use: Pre-calculated caustics textures projected onto surfaces, a common approach that avoids custom shaders.

### C. Projector-component stylized caustics (Unity)
- URL: https://www.patreon.com/posts/23929697 (verified in search results, Patreon post; access may require login)
- Use: Projector component with a stylized caustics texture, triplanar option, and blend modes. Relevant to projected-texture fake caustics.

### D. Content-Driven Multipass Rendering in UE4, Ryan Brucks, GDC 2017
- URL: https://www.unrealengine.com/events/gdc-2017-content-driven-multipass-rendering-in-ue4 (verified, event page; confirm slide/video access on GDC Vault)
- Use: Render-target approach to caustics, volume rendering, and 2D fluid simulation. More technical than stylized, but the caustics texture method transfers to a simpler setup.

### E. Shape language in game character design, Rocketbrush
- URL: https://rocketbrush.com/blog/shape-language-in-game-character-design-how-to-make-characters-readable-and-consistent (verified, blog article)
- Use: Shape-language basics for readable characters. Test readability early, since players rely on silhouette, motion, and quick cues. Colour should support readability, not replace it.

### Gaps
- No single source was found combining low-poly stylization, projected caustics, fog particles, and a small-mobile-screen readability test.
- No authoritative article on creature silhouettes specifically for small mobile screens was found. A thumbnail-scale or silhouette-first test (solid shape with no interior detail should still read) is the closest general practice found.
- Luca (Pixar) item covers simplified forms and clear silhouettes, but the source is press coverage, not a full interview.
- Not searched (no URL collected): Spore, Slime Rancher 2, Fall Guys, Super Mario Odyssey underwater levels, Octonauts, Insaniquarium, Feeding Frenzy, Kirby and the Forgotten Land, Finding Nemo, Ooblets, and the other candidates on the list. Add them in a second pass if needed.
