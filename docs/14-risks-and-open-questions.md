# 14 · Risks and open questions

This chapter lists what could go wrong, what is still unverified, what the owner must decide, where the chapters still disagree, what the plan assumes, and what should stop the project. It is the companion to `13-sprint-plan.md`; sprint numbers, gate IDs (G0 to G10) and dates are from that chapter's base case (30 owner hours a week, soft launch on Thursday 1 April 2027).

Written on Tuesday 13 October 2026. An item leaves this chapter when it is settled: the answer is written into the chapter that owns the subject, and the row here is deleted.

## 6. Stop conditions

Any one of these ends or pauses the project. "Pause" means no new build work until the named thing changes. "Stop" means maintenance as 12 §9 defines it, or no release at all if the game is not yet public.

1. **Spike.** Two or more of spikes S1, S2, S6 and S8 fail in Sprint 0 and no fallback holds 30 fps on device row 1. Stop, or redesign the character with a Humanoid and re-plan from the start.
2. **First two minutes.** Gate G2 fails twice: after two sprints of redesign, fewer than 4 of 8 new players bank at all or fewer than 4 are still playing at 3 minutes. Stop.
3. **Capacity.** After gate G1 the projected soft-launch date is later than 30 September 2027 and the owner can neither raise hours nor pay for help. Pause, and choose between a one-zone build and stopping.
4. **Friends-and-family test.** With 30 or more first-time players, "still playing at 60 seconds" is under 60% or the first-bank rate is under 40%, and one more two-week fix cycle does not lift them above those lines. Do not soft-launch. Pause.
5. **Evaluation.** The 60-day window closes (Monday 31 May 2027 in the base case) without the game passing Roblox's evaluation. Pause; find out whether the window can restart before any more work.
6. **No benchmark.** Daily users cannot be held at 100 for 14 days after paid step 2 is spent and one four-week cycle on the store page and first session. Pause.
7. **No-go.** At gate G7, after two extra four-week cycles or at the 20-week limit (Thursday 19 August 2027), day-1 retention or session time is still below the median for similar games. Stop. The launch scope is not built.
8. **After launch.** Either go/no-go measure is below the 50th percentile for four weeks in a row after two extra cycles; or daily users are below 100 for four weeks in a row; or Earned Robux are below 30,000 a month for three months in a row (12 §9). Maintenance.
9. **Saves.** A bug is damaging saves and cannot be contained with `saves_read_only` within the hour. Revoke public access until it is fixed. This pauses the release, not the project.
10. **Policy.** The maturity label comes back above Moderate, Roblox takes moderation action against the game or the account, or a rule change makes the core loop or its purchases unsellable to the core audience. Pause until resolved.
11. **Name.** Step 1 or step 4 of the name check fails (a Roblox eat-and-grow game named GULP with players, or a live trademark for video games). Pause all marketing and rename in the canon first.
12. **Money.** Cash spent passes the high case of 12 §11 (about 2,150 US dollars) with no cash-out yet. No further spend.
13. **The owner.** The work takes more than 45 hours a week for four weeks in a row. Drop to the keep-steady pace whatever the numbers say (12 §9). If that is not enough, pause.
